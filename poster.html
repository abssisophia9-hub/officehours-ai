Problem Statement

Struggling students rarely seek help the same way. Fear of judgment, slow email threads, and unverified AI hallucinations leave students isolated until exam week, while faculty lack visibility into where students get stuck.

05

AI Pipelines and Guardrails

Ask Triage
AnalyzeMatchSynthesize

Local scikit-learn + MiniLM. An 80-row teaching bank; no TA means the professor.

Never sends student text outside the app
Study Agent
FilterCutoff 0.42Quote check

Approved, same-course material only; refuses below 0.42; exact quote, then entailment (max 3 attempts).

Never answers from unapproved or other-course material
Help Coach
RetrieveSuggest

Drafts from approved material onto a teaching reply; no external model call.

Never publishes; it only drafts for the TA
Pedagogy Agent
SearchRankVerify

YouTube and Wikipedia; at most 3 rounds, 3 model calls, 6 searches, 5 checked links.

Never shows an invented or unchecked link
