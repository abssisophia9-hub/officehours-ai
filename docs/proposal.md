# Project Proposal — OfficeHours AI

## Title
OfficeHours AI: An AI-Assisted Triage System for Finals-Week Academic Support

## Problem Statement
During final exam periods, faculty face a surge of repeated, overlapping, and urgent student requests across email, LMS messages, and office hours. Students, meanwhile, often hesitate to reach out directly due to social anxiety or difficulty articulating their confusion.

## Objectives
- Reduce faculty communication overload during finals week
- Lower the barrier for students to ask for help
- Automatically classify and cluster similar requests
- Recommend appropriate response pathways (FAQ, group session, 1:1, etc.)

## Scope
- Prototype kiosk + dashboard system
- AI/NLP classification and clustering pipeline
- Evaluation via simulated finals-week scenario

## Out of Scope (for this phase)
- Full production deployment
- Integration with university LMS/SIS systems
- Multi-language support (future work)
