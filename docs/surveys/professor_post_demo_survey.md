# Professor Post-Demo Survey

Purpose: evaluate whether OfficeHours AI helps faculty triage student requests,
understand common patterns, and choose appropriate support actions.

Recommended format: short Google Form or interview guide after a dashboard demo.

## Consent / framing

This survey is for a class project evaluation. Please evaluate the workflow and
interface, not real students. Do not include private student information in
free-response answers.

## Suggested success indicators

- Average score of 4 or higher on request queue clarity.
- Faculty agree that clustering/recommendations reduce triage effort.
- Free-response feedback identifies concrete improvements rather than blocking
  concerns about professor control or privacy.
