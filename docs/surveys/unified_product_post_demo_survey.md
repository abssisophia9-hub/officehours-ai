# OfficeHours AI — optional survey templates (not required)

**Capstone decision:** no new survey data needs to be collected before the
final presentation, and surveys are **not** a required deliverable after the
final product. Evaluation for the defense is the live demo + technical
artifacts (classifier / matcher / gold labels / tests).

Templates below are **optional** if someone later wants a Google Form. They
are not a pre-presentation checklist.

**Scale (if used):**  
1 = Strongly disagree · 2 = Disagree · 3 = Neutral · 4 = Agree · 5 = Strongly agree

---

## Optional Form A — Students

1. It was easy to submit a Quiet ask or Identify me from the product app.
2. I understood the difference between anonymous and identified asks.
3. I felt comfortable submitting a question anonymously.
4. The Ask Triage feedback after submit (category / assigned TA / hint) was clear.
5. Checking status with my request code felt private enough.
6. I would be more likely to seek help during finals with this tool.
7. If shown: the Study Agent’s “I don’t know — ask a human” behavior felt honest.
8. Preferred ask mode: `Anonymous` · `Identified` · `It depends`
9. Rooms felt like a classmate forum (not staff watching).
10. I understood that helping in Rooms puts my name on the course leaderboard.
11. Free text: What would you change?
12. Free text: Any privacy or trust concerns?

## Optional Form B — TAs

1. The queue made it clear who needed help and how (message / meeting).
2. Claim → start helping → done / reply felt workable.
3. Help Coach drafts / Socratic steps were useful (or N/A).
4. A shared group meeting for a repeated cluster made sense.
5. It was clear that I am staff on this course, not a classmate in the section.
6. This would reduce repeated 1:1 typing for common questions.
7. Free text: Biggest campus-wide risk?
8. Free text: What would you change in the TA workspace?

## Optional Form C — Professors

1. Clusters and recommendation cards reduced repeated 1:1 handling in the demo.
2. I remained in control of what enters the student Study Agent knowledge base.
3. Canvas materials sync (or local mirror) was understandable for demos.
4. Review insights from Quiet asks + Rooms chapters looked usable for the next session.
5. Shared group meetings for repeated concepts look useful.
6. Perceived workload reduction vs answering each ask separately.
7. I would trust this for a real course after Canvas LTI / campus hardening.
8. Free text: What must be true before a real course?
9. Free text: Ethics / privacy / integrity concerns?
