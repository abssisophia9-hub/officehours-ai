# Student Post-Demo Survey

Purpose: measure whether the OfficeHours AI kiosk feels easy, private, and
comfortable for students during a high-stress finals-week help-seeking moment.

Recommended format: anonymous Google Form or paper form after the demo.

## Consent / framing

This survey is for a class project evaluation. Do not include real grades,
medical information, or sensitive personal details in free-response answers.
Responses should be anonymous.

7. Which submission mode would you prefer for most academic questions?

8. What would make the kiosk feel more trustworthy or comfortable to use?
   - Free response

    - Free response

## Suggested success indicators

- Average score of 4 or higher on ease of use.
- Majority of respondents understand anonymous vs. identified submission.
- Qualitative comments identify privacy/confidence benefits or clear UI fixes.
