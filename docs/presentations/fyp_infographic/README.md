## 60-second pitch

Office hours fail in two directions: staff hear the same stuck point five times, and quiet students will not put a name on a public channel. OfficeHours is one Canvas-linked desk, not a campus chatbot. A student can file a Quiet ask. Ask Triage labels it, names a TA, and copies a past hint. Help Coach stays on the TA screen. The professor stamps the only text the Study Agent may quote. Scan the QR for a phone prototype of the vision — Sharing notes, Rooms, and the professor stamp.
