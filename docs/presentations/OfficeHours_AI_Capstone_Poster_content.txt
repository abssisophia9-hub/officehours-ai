PROJECT OVERVIEW
Finals-week ask overload across email/Canvas. OfficeHours AI triages asks, staffs TAs, and gates Study Agent answers behind professor stamp—local NLP, Canvas-first, not an open chatbot.
• Context / application area: AUI courses; students, TAs, professors at peak support demand.
• Proposed idea: React + FastAPI app with Ask Triage, Help Coach, stamped Study Agent, Rooms, Canvas LTI/sync.
• Expected contribution: Course-scoped triage, dual-TA staffing, KB stamp gate, demo→live Canvas path.
PROBLEM DEFINITION & RELEVANCE
Lower ask barriers and repeat load while keeping faculty control of AI answers.
• Problem statement / research question: How can office hours cut repeat load and ask friction without unconstrained AI?
• Stakeholders / intended users: Students (Quiet ask, Study Agent, Rooms); TAs (queue, Help Coach); Professors (KB stamp).
• Evidence, scope & constraints: Silver/synthetic labels; local ML default; live Canvas needs IT + HTTPS; no portal scraping.
RELATED WORK, GAP & PROPOSED INNOVATION
LMS inboxes/forums vs a gated triage product with dual-TA staffing.
• Existing work / alternatives: LMS inbox; Piazza/Ed; tutoring chatbots; FAQ bots without stamp or dual-TA match.
• Gap / unmet need: No Quiet escalation + local triage ML + dual-TA staffing + professor stamp before student AI.
• Proposed innovation: Ask Triage + Help Coach + stamped Study Agent; Canvas LTI; trackable Quiet asks.
OPTIONAL VISUAL: LMS inbox vs gated pipeline • Quiet ask → My asks reply
