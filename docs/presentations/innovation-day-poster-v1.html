OfficeHours AI

Campus office-hours triage for finals week — one app, role per course.

Innovation Day
Poster v1

Problem: Finals week floods faculty with repeated questions, while some students hesitate to ask for help.

What breaks today

  • Same conceptual questions reappear across the queue
  • Long waits; quiet students stay stuck alone
  • TAs reinvent answers under pressure
  • Approved course knowledge never compounds

Physical kiosk hardware is out of scope. The product is a packaged multi-role web app with Canvas-ready launch.

The product

Student Quiet ask or Identify me · message / face-to-face / online · Rooms · Study Agent
TA Live queue · claim/help · Help Coach while teaching · drafts for curation
Professor Repeated topics · review insights · approve KB · Canvas materials sync
One login · membership decides role per course
