OfficeHours AI

AI-Assisted Campus Office-Hours Triage · One App · Role Per Course

Sophia Abssi BSc AI and Robotization Graduating Fall 2026 Final Year Capstone Project

Problem Statement

  • Finals week floods faculty with repeated conceptual questions
  • Students hesitate to ask — especially when identity feels exposed
  • TA answers are reinvented under pressure; approved knowledge rarely compounds

Research Objectives

  • Can local Ask Triage reduce faculty communication overload at intake?
  • Can Quiet ask improve comfort seeking academic help?
  • Can Matcher + dual-TA staffing cut wait / misroute cost?
  • Can a professor KB gate keep Study Agent answers trustworthy?
  • Can Canvas LTI replace manual enrollment for community deploy?

Roles (Per Course)

  • Student — Quiet / Identify · ask modes · status · Rooms · Notes · Study Agent
  • TA — live queue · claim/help · Help Coach · drafts · group meetings
  • Professor — clusters · review insights · KB approve · Canvas sync

Unique Value Proposition

  • One packaged multi-role app — not three disconnected dashboards
  • Role per course — same login can be TA on 2302 & student elsewhere
  • Quiet ask privacy path + named Rooms for peer help
  • Custom-trained Ask Triage + Help Coach + professor KB gate
  • Canvas-ready launch / modules / materials (LTI 1.3 path)
  • Hardware kiosk / NFC intentionally out of scope
