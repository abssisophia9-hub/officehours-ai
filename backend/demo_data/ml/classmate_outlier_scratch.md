# Classmate scratch: a point that looks extreme

Demo. Scratch comparison I have not shown the professor. Marker violetscratchtoken.

I picked a buoy with a huge height and a normal wind. Euclidean distance said it was far from the average. After I thought about the tilt of the cloud, I was less sure. If height and wind rise together, that point might sit along the long axis of the ellipse.

I do not have a cutoff of my own. This scratch file is only the picture I drew, not a rule for calling something an outlier.
